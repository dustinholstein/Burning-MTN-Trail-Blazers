About the Group

We're a small, informal group of friends who love getting outside. Every Wednesday evening we meet up for trail running, hiking, or biking in and around the Burning Mountain area. No pressure, no paces — just good company on good trails.

⛰ Activities

Trail running, hiking, and biking through some of the best terrain the valley has to offer.

☰ Our Routes

Burning Mountain area, Alder Park, Lakota, and surrounding trails. We mix it up every week.

⏱ Duration

Activities typically last about 60–75 minutes — enough to get a great workout in before sunset.

☕ After the Trail

We always meet up at Down Valley Brewing afterward. Grab your own food — tacos, pizza, whatever sounds good.

Group Rules

A few simple guidelines to keep things safe and fun for everyone.

  • 1 Stay together. Everyone stays with the group — no running ahead and leaving others behind.
  • 2 No pressure. Participation is flexible. No obligation to show up every single week.
  • 3 Come prepared. Bring water and wear appropriate clothing for the conditions.
  • 4 Dogs on leash. Dogs are welcome but must be on-leash at all times.
  • 5 Respect boundaries. Stay on designated trails and respect all trail boundaries and private property — no trespassing.
  • 6 Trail designations. Do not hike on mountain-bike-only designated trails.
  • 7 Biking routes. Biking routes include Alder Park and Lakota. Avoid Medaris and Prendergast — they're too steep.
